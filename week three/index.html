WHY OPEN PAGES?

Good information should feel open to everyone.

Open Pages is a fictional information portal concept. This demo is built around readable content, clear structure, predictable controls, and keyboard-friendly navigation. Accessibility is an ongoing practice, not a final checkbox.

ACCESSIBILITY NOTE

Accessibility begins before the visuals.

Meaningful headings, descriptive links, a logical reading order, and visible keyboard focus help people navigate content in different ways. Use this page as a small example, then test real content with assistive technologies and people with varied access needs.
